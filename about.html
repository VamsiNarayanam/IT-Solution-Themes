Our evolution

From a focused product studio to a full-spectrum engineering partner—the milestones that shaped how we deliver today.

  1. 2014

    Founded as a product studio

    Stackly began as a small team shipping web and API products for early-stage companies—obsessed with clean architecture and honest delivery dates.

  2. 2017

    Cloud & DevOps practice

    We formalized infrastructure automation, CI/CD, and observability so clients could scale traffic without scaling chaos—Kubernetes, cloud landing zones, and release engineering became core offerings.

  3. 2020

    Enterprise & regulated workloads

    Deeper work in healthcare, fintech, and logistics pushed us to mature security reviews, audit trails, and compliance-aware design—without slowing product velocity.

  4. 2023

    Global squads & design partnership

    We scaled cross-functional pods across time zones and embedded product design so roadmaps stayed user-centered from discovery through launch and iteration.

  5. 2026

    Today: outcome-led engineering

    Stackly operates as a delivery partner measured on releases, uptime, and business impact—pairing modern tooling with the same transparency and craft we started with.
